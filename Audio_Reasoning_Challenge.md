# Audio Reasoning Challenge (Interspeech 2026)

This document summarizes the key aspects of the Audio Reasoning Challenge, as detailed on its official website.

## Overview

The Audio Reasoning Challenge, scheduled for Interspeech 2026, is designed to push the boundaries of Large Audio Language Models (LALMs). The primary goal is to move beyond simple audio perception and foster the development of models capable of robust and interpretable reasoning.

## Key Features

*   **Focus on Reasoning:** Unlike traditional benchmarks, this challenge emphasizes the model's ability to reason about audio, not just perceive it.
*   **MMAR Benchmark:** It utilizes the enriched MMAR benchmark, which now includes Chain-of-Thought (CoT) annotations. This allows for a more detailed evaluation of the model's reasoning process.
*   **Strict Evaluation:** For a prediction to be considered correct, both the reasoning path (the "how") and the final answer (the "what") must be accurate.
*   **Two Tracks:** The challenge is divided into two distinct tracks:
    *   **Single Model Track:** This track focuses on the intrinsic reasoning capabilities of a single, self-contained model.
    *   **Agent Track:** This track is designed for evaluating system-level orchestration and the use of external tools to solve audio-based problems.
